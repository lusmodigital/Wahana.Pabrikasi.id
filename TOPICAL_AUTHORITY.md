# Topical Authority — wahana.pabrikasi.id

## Role and boundary

`wahana.pabrikasi.id` is a specialist knowledge and commercial-support property for fabricated children's and family amusement rides. It should help venue owners, tourism operators, schools, event businesses, and procurement teams choose, specify, install, operate, inspect, and maintain appropriate rides.

Transactional product pages own availability, quotation, custom fabrication, delivery, and ordering. Editorial pages explain decisions and operating risks without duplicating those sales routes. Exact safety, structural, electrical, capacity, and inspection claims require product-specific engineering evidence and qualified review.

## Context used

- Homepage: “Pusat Wahana Permainan Anak Hiburan,” with product, service, custom-design, delivery, and company sections.
- Repository: 9,719 tracked files.
- Sitemap evidence: 45 XML sitemap files with 8,413 `<loc>` entries.
- Meaningful product/category signals: bom-bom car, istana balon, kereta mini (rel, motor, mobil), kincir ria, komidi putar, mainan koin, mainan pegas, mandi bola, mobil goes, mobil wisata, odong-odong, perahu handboat, sepeda air, and waterboom slide.
- Meaningful route families: product pages, 18 product categories, 40 news/archive pages, company/contact pages, and custom-build/service routes.
- Live homepage and sitemap were not reliably reachable during planning; repository-export evidence is the current context source and live parity remains a deployment gate.

## Ignored template noise

- 8,340 root-level `jual-*.html` location-swapped sales pages.
- Category pagination, 40 news pagination pages, author archives, `uncategorized`, feeds, WordPress assets, cache/build files, and repeated metadata.
- Automotive-diagnostic template remnants such as `computer-diagnostic`, `fuel-system-repair`, and `electro-diagnostics`; these do not define the amusement-ride topical scope.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent topic hub. Requirement pages link to the relevant product-family hub; product-family pages link to site planning, procurement, installation, operation, and maintenance. Diagnostic pages link to the applicable stop-use rule, inspection step, repair decision, and replacement/procurement page. Commercial routes receive links only where a reader has enough information to request a product-specific proposal.
